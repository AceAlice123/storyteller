In the nineteenth century, Charles Babbage conceptualized the Analytical Engine, laying the mathematical foundation for modern programmable computation.

Decades later, Alan Turing introduced the concept of universal machines, breaking wartime ciphers and pioneering artificial intelligence.

The invention of the silicon microprocessor enabled computers to shrink from room-sized mainframes into ubiquitous personal devices.

Modern deep neural networks have transformed artificial intelligence, empowering systems to understand language, perception, and complex visual patterns.

Today, multimodal architectures bridge text and vision, unlocking automated creative synthesis and intelligent digital storytelling.
